Features

Everything you need to succeed

From registration to results, every step of the academic lifecycle is covered in a single, integrated system.

  • Course Management

    Browse and enrol in courses, view syllabi, and track your academic progress across all registered modules.

  • Attendance Tracking

    Lecturers can record and review attendance in real time. Students stay informed about their own attendance standing.

  • Assignments

    Submit assignments, view deadlines, and receive feedback — all from a centralised assignments dashboard.

  • Results & Grades

    Access semester results, grade breakdowns, and academic transcripts securely at any time.

  • Student Profiles

    Maintain personal details, academic history, and account settings through a secure profile page.

  • Secure & Reliable

    Role-based access ensures students, lecturers, and admins only see what they are authorised to view.
